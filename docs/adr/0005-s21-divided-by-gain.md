# S21 is divided by the gain, and conjugated on the lower sideband

Every measurement turns the readout's I + jQ into S21 with `received_s21`: divided by the readout tone's gain (as QICK rounded it), and complex-conjugated when the setup uses the lower sideband (RF = LO − IF), where the mixer mirrors the chip's phase. A PNA-X divides by its reference receiver (S21 = b2/a1) so the trace does not move with source power; we have no reference receiver, but the DAC is linear in gain (−6.02 dB for half the gain on the bench loopback), so the gain plays the role of a1. Saved `s21` arrays from before this change (commit f0080f8 and earlier) are in plain ADC units and keep the lower-sideband phase mirrored. We decided against normalizing to a stored reference trace (a PNA-X response cal): no thru is possible inside the cryostat, and the quantities we use (resonance frequency, width, depth, Q) come from the resonance's shape.
